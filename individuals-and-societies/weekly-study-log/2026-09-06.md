## What I Learned

This week in I&S, I learned about different political systems and political positions.

For example, in Iran, the Supreme Leader has the highest political authority, and the Guardian Council has an important role in deciding who is allowed to run as a candidate.

In Denmark, there is still a royal family, but the monarch has very limited political power. Most political power is exercised by the elected government and the prime minister.

We also discussed the political spectrum and tried to place famous political figures, such as Gandhi and Hitler, on the left-wing or right-wing side depending on their ideas and beliefs.

## What Went Well

I understood the basic differences between political systems quite well, especially the idea that some countries have powerful unelected institutions while others give most political power to elected governments.

## Where I Got Stuck

I found it difficult to decide which countries and political figures belong on the left or right of the political spectrum because I do not follow politics very closely.

I also realised that some people do not fit perfectly into just one side, because they may have different views on different issues.

## How I Dealt With It

When I was unsure, I asked Dr. Matthew, our World Politics teacher, for clarification.

He helped explain some of the disagreements about where different countries and political figures should be placed on the political spectrum.
