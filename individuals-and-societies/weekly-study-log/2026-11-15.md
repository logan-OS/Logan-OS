### What did I learn this week?

This week in I&S, we learned about the Milgram experiment. It was a psychology experiment that tested how far people would go when following orders from someone in authority. The participants were told to give electric shocks to another person when they answered questions incorrectly. The shocks were not real, but the participants did not know that.
