### What did I learn this week?

This week in I&S, we continued learning about human behaviour and the Milgram experiment. We also took an online test about racial bias and were asked to reflect on our results.

### What did I find surprising?

My test result suggested that I did not have the racial bias being measured. I was quite surprised because I thought I might have some biases. For example, I sometimes have negative opinions about Americans, even though I share a similar racial background with some of them.

### What changed my thinking?

I started questioning where my opinions came from. I think some of my views about Americans may have been influenced by my father's opinions. This made me wonder whether my beliefs come from my own experiences or from the people around me.

### What question do I still have?

Can people develop prejudices without realising it? I also wonder how much our family and environment influence the way we judge other people.

### What do I want to improve?

I want to become better at questioning my own assumptions and forming opinions based on my own experiences and evidence rather than automatically accepting what other people tell me.
