### What did I learn this week?

This week in English, we practised comparing and contrasting two short stories, *Fish Cheeks* by Amy Tan and *How to Be Chinese*. Both texts explore cultural identity, belonging, and the difficulties of fitting into society.

### What did I find interesting?

I found it interesting that the characters struggle with their cultural identity because my own experience is quite different. As someone with a mixed cultural background, I feel that I can belong in different cultures rather than feeling that I belong nowhere.

### What was challenging?

The challenging part was understanding why the characters felt embarrassed or uncomfortable about their cultural backgrounds. Personally, I think confidence plays an important role in how people express their identity. However, I also realise that other people may have different experiences from mine.

### What question do I still have?

Does belonging depend more on a person's confidence or on how society accepts them? I also wonder why some people see their cultural differences as a weakness while others see them as a strength.

### What do I want to improve?

I want to become better at comparing and contrasting texts, using quotations to support my arguments, and understanding perspectives that are different from my own.
