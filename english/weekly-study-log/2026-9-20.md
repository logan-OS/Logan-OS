# 📖 English Weekly Study Log

## What I Learned

This week in English, I learned that images and objects can have meanings that were not originally intended by the creator.

For example, I put an eye in my collage mainly because I thought it looked good, but other people interpreted it as a symbol of wisdom. This made me realise that an audience can interpret the same image differently from the person who created it.

## What Went Well

My presentation about my collage went very smoothly. I felt confident explaining my choices and responding when people asked about different parts of the collage.

## Where I Got Stuck

I did not really get stuck during this task.

However, I noticed that sometimes I created explanations for objects after I had already put them in the collage.

For example, I originally added seashells because I liked how they looked, but during the presentation I explained them as representing my love of the sea.

## What I Learned From That

I realised there is a difference between finding a possible interpretation of something and pretending that the meaning was intentional from the beginning.

In future work, I should be clearer about which symbols I chose intentionally and which meanings I only noticed afterwards.

## What I Need to Improve Next Week

Next week, I want to make my analysis more genuine by thinking about the meaning of my choices while I am creating the work, instead of creating explanations afterwards.
