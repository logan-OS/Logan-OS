## What I Noticed Differently This Time

Because I had already read *The Paper Menagerie* before, I was able to notice details that I missed the first time.

This time, I noticed that the paper used to fold the menagerie connects different parts of the family's culture. I interpreted this as a symbol of the bond between the two cultures in the family.

When I first read the story last year, I mainly focused on the plot. Reading it again made me pay more attention to symbolism and why the author chose certain details.
