### What did I learn this week?

This week in English, we analysed short stories. One of the stories I studied was *Fish Cheeks* by Amy Tan. The story is about a Chinese American girl who feels embarrassed about her culture when an American boy she likes comes to her house for Christmas Eve dinner. I learned how the story explores cultural identity and the pressure to fit into society.

### What did I practise?

I practised analysing the characters, conflicts, themes, and important quotes in *Fish Cheeks*. I also made a chart to organise my ideas and understand the story better.

### What was challenging?

The challenging part was understanding the deeper meaning of the story. At first, I didn't really understand why the story was called *Fish Cheeks*. After learning more about it, I understood that the fish cheeks represent Amy's Chinese culture and how she feels embarrassed about it.
