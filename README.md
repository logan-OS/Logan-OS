# 🧠 Latest Reflection — The Phantom Foul

**Date:** January 24, 2026

## 🔋 Status Check

**Current Mood:** Frustrated — it felt like we were robbed of a win.

**Highlight of the Week:** Beating MAK in the semi-finals with back-to-back three-pointers. Pure clutch.

**Challenge of the Week:** Dealing with a disputed foul call in the final against MAT.

## Tournament Recap

January 24 was the TES Basketball Tournament. We fought hard to reach the final.

We beat MAK in the semi-final, including two important three-pointers in a row.

The final against MAT was extremely close. We stayed within one point for much of the game.

In the last minute, the referee called a foul that we believed we had not committed. Our coach argued the decision, but the call remained.

MAT received three free throws, creating a four-point gap with only 16 seconds remaining. We could not recover and lost the final.

## 🔍 What I Learned About Myself

I realised that losing because another team played better feels very different from losing when I believe an important decision was unfair.

Fairness matters a lot to me, especially in competition. At the same time, this experience reminded me that I cannot control every decision or outcome. I can control how I respond, how I support my teammates, and how I learn from the experience.
