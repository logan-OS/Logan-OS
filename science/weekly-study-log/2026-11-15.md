### What did I learn this week?

This week in Science, I learned about titration. I learned that titration is a method used to find the concentration of an unknown solution by slowly adding another solution with a known concentration. We also learned how indicators can change colour when the solution reaches a certain pH.

### What did I find interesting?

I found it interesting that a small amount of liquid can cause the solution to change colour. I also learned that titration needs to be done carefully because adding too much liquid can affect the results.

### What was challenging?

The challenging part was understanding how to know when to stop adding the solution and how to identify the endpoint of the titration.

### What question do I still have?

How can we tell exactly when a titration is complete, and why do different indicators change colour at different pH levels?

### What do I want to improve?

I want to understand titration better, especially how to read measurements accurately and calculate the concentration of an unknown solution. I also want to become more confident with chemistry experiments.
