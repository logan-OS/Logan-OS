# 🔬 Science Weekly Study Log

**Week:** 31 August – 6 September 2026

## What I Learned

This week in Science, I learned about the different states of matter and how particles behave in solids, liquids, and gases.

When temperature increases, particles gain more energy and move faster. In gases, faster-moving particles can create greater pressure because they collide with the walls of the container more often and with more force.

I also learned that particles in liquids are close together but can move past one another, which allows liquids such as water to flow. In solids, the particles are held in fixed positions and mainly vibrate, so solids keep their shape.

We also reviewed changes of state such as freezing and evaporation.

## What Went Well

The changes of state were easier for me to understand because I had already learned about the water cycle.

Thinking about what water particles are doing during freezing and evaporation helped me understand the particle model more easily.

## Where I Got Stuck

I got confused when we discussed mercury.

I originally thought substances would have freezing and boiling points similar to water, but mercury has very different values.

## How I Dealt With It

I asked my teacher about it.

The teacher explained that different substances have their own melting/freezing points and boiling points. This helped me understand that water is only one example and should not be used as the rule for every substance.

## What I Need to Improve Next Week

Next week, I want to understand whether there are patterns in the periodic table that can help explain why different substances have different melting and boiling points.

Instead of memorising every value separately, I want to understand what causes the differences.
